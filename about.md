# Mega Hack Installer

Mega Hack is Geometry Dash's best-known and longest-running mod, now available on Geode. Featuring over 160 unique mods to customise your Geometry Dash experience to your own liking.

Press TAB to open the menu on Windows, or the Mega Hack floating icon to open on Android.
